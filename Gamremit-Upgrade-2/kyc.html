📸 How to take a good selfie
  • Hold your document next to your face, clearly visible
  • Make sure your full face and the document are in the same photo
  • Ensure good lighting — no shadows on your face
  • Look directly at the camera with a neutral expression
  • Do not use glasses, hats or masks that obscure your face
  • The document text must be readable in the photo
